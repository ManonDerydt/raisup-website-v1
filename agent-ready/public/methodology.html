How we score

The Agent‑Ready Score shows whether AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude) can find, understand, recommend and book your business. It is a guide, not a guarantee: AI answers change from day to day.

Overall score

60% site readiness (what your website gives AI) + 40% AI visibility (what assistants say about you today). If we cannot ask the assistants, the score equals site readiness.

AI visibility

We ask each assistant the same question a client would ask: which salons it recommends in your city, and what it knows about you. Points: recommended (40), knows you (15), gives your real website (15), gives a booking link (10), knows your prices (10), knows your hours (10).

What we read

Only public pages: your homepage, robots.txt, llms.txt and sitemap.xml. Our scanner identifies itself as AgentReadyScanner.

Agent‑Ready is independent and not affiliated with OpenAI, Google, Perplexity or Anthropic.
