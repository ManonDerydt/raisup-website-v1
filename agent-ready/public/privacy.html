Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Public business information: website address, business name, city, public website content.
  • If you choose to give it: your email address, to send your report and a re-check.
  • Basic technical logs (IP address for rate limiting), kept 30 days.

Why

To produce your score and report, send what you asked for, and prevent abuse. We do not sell personal data.

Who processes it

Hosting provider; AI providers (OpenAI, Google, Perplexity) receive only the business name, city and category to ask what they know; email provider.

Your rights

You can ask to access or delete your data at any time (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA). Every email includes an unsubscribe link.

Retention

Reports and emails are kept up to 24 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
