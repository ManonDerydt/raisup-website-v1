Questions

Plain answers.

Do I need a web developer?

No for most fixes. We generate everything and show you exactly where to paste it. On Wix, Squarespace and WordPress it takes about 5 minutes. Or we host it for you.

Is this the same as SEO?

It’s related but different. SEO helps you rank in Google’s list of links. Agent‑Ready helps AI assistants give your salon as the answer, with correct prices, hours and a booking link.

How do you know what AI says about me?

We ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the same questions your clients ask, and show you their answers with the date. Answers can change from one day to the next, so treat the score as a guide, not a guarantee.

What is an llms.txt file?

A short page written for AI assistants: your services, prices, hours and booking link. Think of it as your menu, for robots.

Are you affiliated with OpenAI or Google?

No. Agent‑Ready is independent. ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity are trademarks of their owners.
