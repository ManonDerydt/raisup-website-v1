[PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/brightlocal-launches-ai-insights-to-help-businesses-navigate-increasingly-complex-local-search-302736885.html); [BrightLocal blog](https://www.brightlocal.com/blog/introducing-ai-insights/)

BrightLocal's own agencies page says "Trusted by 6,000+ SEO Agencies". [BrightLocal home](https://www.brightlocal.com/); [BrightLocal agencies](https://www.brightlocal.com/agencies/)

## Q2. BrightLocal consumer survey (LCRS 2026): 45% / 6% / 23% / 18% / 75%

### Takeaway
**PARTLY TRUE.**
- **Confirmed:** 45% of consumers used AI for a local recommendation, up from 6% the year before (n=1,002 US adults).
- **Probably garbled:** "23% in most recent local search". The 23% I found is the share who used *Google AI Mode* for recommendations.
- **Probably garbled:** "only 18% would contact directly". The 18% I found is "skip over [AI review] summaries entirely".
- **Unverified:** "75% verify on Google". BrightLocal reports that 88% of AI users verify or double-check before acting.

### Cited Findings
- 6% (2025) → 45% (2026). AI is now the third most-used source for local recommendations, after Google and Facebook. [BrightLocal LCRS AI trust](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/lcrs-ai-trust/); [BrightLocal LCRS 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)
- Sample: 1,002 US adults via SurveyMonkey. [DesignRush summary](https://news.designrush.com/ai-local-business-discovery-website-strategy); [Aspen Daily News](https://www.aspendailynews.com/features/article_b921b781-a6fd-5051-b5e2-af787dd56850-47ab237f)
- ChatGPT was used by 31% for recommendations and Google AI Mode by 23%. 64% of 30–44-year-olds have used AI. Google's share as top review platform fell from 83% to 71%. (Same sources as above.)
- "88% of AI users … verify sources (37%) or check if the review is legitimate (51%)". "Just 18% skip over summaries entirely". [Bill Hartzer](https://www.billhartzer.com/local-search/ai-local-business-recommendations-45-percent/) (snippet)
- A secondary source headlines "47% of U.S. Adults Used AI for Local Search" and discusses what happens after the recommendation. I could not fetch it to check the 18%/75% figures. [Rohring Results](https://rohringresults.com/ai-local-search-consumer-behavior-businesses/)

### Inferences
- The consumer behavior backs the "recommended" half of the thesis. But the verify-before-acting finding (88%) means the booking step still usually happens outside the AI today.

### Gaps
- Exact publication month (the claim says March 2026). The LCRS is usually released in Q1, but I did not confirm the date.
- A primary source for the 23% / 18% / 75% figures as the claim frames them.

## Q11. Who already does "AI booking readiness", "AI booking connector" or agentic booking for local services?

### Takeaway
The connector layer is being taken by the booking platforms themselves:
- Fresha: live in ChatGPT and Claude.
- Square: ChatGPT app and Claude plugin. Restaurants are live; appointments are "coming soon".
- Booksy, Fresha and Vagaro: partners for Google AI Mode agentic booking.
- Calendly, EasyWeek, Calendesk and Bella Booking: MCP servers.

Google itself now does agentic booking for beauty, and at I/O 2026 it added AI calling local businesses on a customer's behalf. The readiness audit layer has only small, unfunded players (AgentHermes free score, TiltStack, Capconvert Cortex, AI Page Ready). I found **no funded startup doing end-to-end "can an AI agent actually book this business" testing, sold white-label to agencies.**

### Cited Findings
**Platforms and infrastructure**
- Google AI Mode agentic booking:
  - Beauty and wellness appointments and event tickets (Search Labs, US). Partners include Booksy, Fresha and Vagaro. Announced August 2025, expanded November 2025. [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/googles-ai-mode-adds-agentic-booking-for-tickets); [Booksy blog](https://biz.booksy.com/blog/booksy-google-ai-mode-integration)
  - At I/O 2026, Google extended agentic booking to local services (home repair, beauty, pet care) and can call businesses. [Capconvert](https://www.capconvert.com/learn/blog/google-agentic-booking-local-services); [Partoo](https://www.partoo.co/en/blog/google-io-2026/)
- Square (1 July 2026): ChatGPT app and Claude plugin. Automatic enrollment, no extra commission. Food and beverage live; "services and appointment-based businesses coming soon". [Square press](https://squareup.com/us/en/press/claude-chatgpt-integrations); [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/07/01/square-agentic-commerce-integrations-chatgpt-claude/)

- No official ChatGPT or MCP connector for Booksy or GlossGenius, per a third party. [usecarly Booksy](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/booksy-ai/); [usecarly GlossGenius](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/glossgenius-ai/)

**Booking-software MCP servers**

- [Bella Booking](https://bellabooking.com/developers) (salon and spa API plus managed MCP)

**Readiness audits**

- AI Page Ready: a white-label AEO audit widget for agencies. It covers visibility, not booking. [aipageready.com](https://aipageready.com/)
- Agentic Calendars explains Google agentic booking for small businesses. [agenticcalendars.com](https://agenticcalendars.com/blog/what-is-agentic-booking/)

**Adjacent funded players**
- Zoca: $6M led by Accel. AI marketing agents for salons and wellness businesses, including visibility on Google, Maps and ChatGPT. [Zoca funding](https://zoca.com/funding-announcement); [Indian Startup News](https://indianstartupnews.com/funding/ai-startup-zoca-raises-6-million-in-a-funding-round-led-by-accel-others-9176217)
- Commerce infrastructure: Nekuda ($5M seed, Madrona) and Firmly ($5.2M). [Rye landscape](https://rye.com/blog/agentic-commerce-startups)

### Inferences (blunt)
- **Closed to a tiny team:**
  - Generic AI-visibility monitoring (Profound, Peec, Search Atlas, BrightLocal, Local Falcon, all cheap or well funded and already selling to agencies).
  - Building your own booking connector or MCP for salons. The platforms (Fresha, Square, Booksy, Vagaro, Google) own the calendar and inventory and are shipping it themselves.
- **Still open, but narrow:**
  1. A "bookability QA" layer. It would run real agent tests to answer: can ChatGPT, Claude or Google AI Mode actually go from recommending this salon to booking it? It would flag the breakpoints (booking platform not agent-enabled, GBP not linked to a reserve partner, services and prices not machine-readable, wrong hours) and give a fix list. It would be sold white-label to local-SEO agencies as an add-on to their BrightLocal or Local Falcon stack.
  2. A migration and configuration service: "get this salon onto an agent-enabled platform or partner and set it up correctly".
- **Main risks:**
  - It is a feature, not a platform. BrightLocal or Local Falcon could add a "bookable by AI: yes/no" check in a sprint.
  - Google and Fresha may make bookability automatic for businesses on their rails.
  - Agent booking volume for independent US salons outside Fresha, Booksy and Vagaro is unproven.
  - Consumers still verify before booking (88% of AI users verify or double-check).
- **Timing:** there is a window of maybe 6–12 months while agent booking is fragmented across Google, ChatGPT and Claude and across platforms. The durable asset would be a dataset of which platforms and configurations actually get booked by which agent. A tiny team could build that faster than incumbents care to.

### Gaps
- There is no public data on actual agent-completed booking volumes for US salons.
- I could not confirm whether Vagaro, Mindbody or GlossGenius have ChatGPT or Claude apps. None were found as of 2026-09-28.
- Search coverage of very small stealth startups is weak. Product Hunt and Y Combinator (YC) batch lists were not checked.
