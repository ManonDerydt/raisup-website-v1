## Q1. Offer A: white-label "AI bookability audit" for US local-marketing agencies ($149 for 5 audits, then ~$299/mo)

### Takeaway
There is real demand: US agencies already resell "AI visibility" to local clients at $500–1,500/client/month. But the white-label tool shelf is crowded and cheap, and a lot of it costs $149/mo for hundreds of brands. A $149 audit pack from an unknown French vendor competes on price with SaaS that does more. What still sets Agent-Ready apart is the **bookability/agentic** angle, which has become concrete since Google rolled out agentic booking in AI Mode worldwide (April 2026) and extended it to local services at I/O 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Agencies charge $500–1,500 for entry-level AI visibility audits for small local businesses and $2,500–5,000+ for multi-location clients. Monitoring retainers run $500–2,000/mo, and resellers charge $500–1,500 per client per month [snippet] — [Respona](https://respona.com/blog/white-label-ai-visibility-services/); [Routeless](https://www.routeless.io/blog/white-label-ai-visibility-reports); [NiceDigitals](https://www.nicedigitals.com/how-to-offer-ai-visibility-audits-as-an-agency-service/)
- Competing white-label tools: Rank Prompt Agency $149/mo for 500 brands with white-label reports; Ayzeo Pro $149/mo with white-label PDFs; Otterly.AI $29/$189/$489/mo; Profound from $99/mo [snippet] — [Rank Prompt](https://rankprompt.com/best-ai-visibility-tools-for-agencies/); [Ayzeo](https://ayzeo.com/blog/white-label-ai-visibility-platform-for-agencies); [LLM Pulse agencies](https://llmpulse.ai/solutions/agencies)
- White Label IQ already sells an "AI Visibility Audit for Agencies" as a done-for-you white-label service — [White Label IQ](https://www.whitelabeliq.com/white-label-website-audit-services/ai-visibility-audit/)
- Google made agentic restaurant booking in AI Mode available worldwide on 10 April 2026, with no subscription required, through partners such as OpenTable and SevenRooms — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-mode-agentic-restaurant-booking/); [9to5Google, 2026-04-10](https://9to5google.com/2026/04/10/google-ai-mode-redesign/)

### Inferences
- **[estimate]** Time to first cash is 2–4 weeks if cold email to agencies starts now. $149 is an impulse buy, but agency owners get a lot of cold email about AI-visibility tools. Realistic 30-day revenue: $0–1,000 (for example 2–5 packs at $149 plus perhaps 1–2 conversions to $299/mo). Recurring revenue beyond that takes more than 30 days.
- **[estimate]** Her edge is narrow but real: "can an AI agent **book** you" (booking links, Reserve with Google/OpenTable presence, opening hours, JSON-LD `potentialAction`/`ReservationAction`) differs from the "are you mentioned" trackers. She should pitch it that way, not as generic GEO.
- Main risks: the product is commoditised (open-source plus $149/mo tools), there is no US track record, and US time-zone sales calls are hard.

## Q2. Offer B: done-for-you audit and fixes for French/European local businesses or French web agencies, in French

### Takeaway
Demand for "GEO / visibilité ChatGPT" is visible in France in 2026: many agencies sell it, agencies publish price lists, and there are press and government guides (francenum.gouv.fr). French agencies list GEO audits at **€1,240–5,000** and retainers from **€350/mo** (small businesses) to **€990+/mo** (SMEs). That leaves room for a €290–690 fixed-price "audit + fixes installed" offer. Local French TPEs are slow, price-sensitive buyers. **French web agencies** (white-label, in French) and **B2B startups/SMEs** are faster buyers.

## Q3. Offer C: non-dilutive funding dossiers (Bpifrance BFT, i-Nov, regional aid, CIR/CII/JEI, EIC)

### Takeaway
The market is large and the price norms are clear: CIR/CII consultants take **15–20% of the credit** for SMEs, usually with a retainer. But **success fees pay out when the money arrives**, which is typically 1–4+ months after a grant decision, or after the tax filing for CIR. That makes success-fee-only work **incompatible with a 30-day cash goal**. Only a **fixed fee** (for example "dossier Bourse French Tech rédigé": about €1.5–3k [estimate]) fits. The sector is crowded and seen as "suspect" when fees are success-only.

### Cited Findings
- CIR/CII consultant fees for SMEs are typically 15–20% of the credit obtained. A 10% success fee actually costs about 13% after the tax-base effect, and 20% costs about 26% [snippet, site blocked] — [Compass Financement, CIR tarifs 2026](https://compass-financement.com/cir-cii-comparatif-tarifs-cabinets-conseil/)
- The BOFiP (tax administration doctrine) has specific rules for how CIR consultant fees affect the credit calculation — [BOFiP BOI-BIC-RICI-10-10-30-20](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/4680-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-BIC-RICI-10-10-30-20-20210713)
- Success fees are usually paired with a fixed upfront retainer that is kept regardless of outcome. Consulting-trade press reports that "success fees n'ont pas la cote" — [Bpifrance Création](https://bpifrance-creation.fr/success-fee); [Consultor](https://www.consultor.fr/articles/les-success-fees-n-ont-pas-la-cote); [Winbids](https://www.winbids.fr/modeles-de-remuneration-des-cabinets-de-conseil-en-innovation/)
- Bourse French Tech: up to €30k (average ticket about €25k, max €50k in some cases), up to 70% of expenses. Instruction takes 2–4 months, payment 1–3 months after that, in two tranches (70% at signature, 30% later) — [Bpifrance](https://www.bpifrance.fr/catalogue-offres/bourse-french-tech); [Infogreffe](https://mesaidespubliques.infogreffe.fr/aides/bourse-french-tech); [Legalstart](https://www.legalstart.fr/fiches-pratiques/aides-creation-entreprise/bourse-french-tech/). *Conflict:* Winbids describes €30k–90k, which is probably outdated or mixed with Emergence/other schemes — [Winbids](https://www.winbids.fr/bourse-french-tech/)
- External consulting services, including business-plan help, can count as eligible BFT expenses. So her fee could partly be financed by the grant, but only after the award — [Winbids](https://www.winbids.fr/bourse-french-tech/)
- Many competing firms: Myriad, BirdInnov, Acsio, Finalli, Compass, plus Winbids as a consultant marketplace — [BirdInnov](https://birdinnov.com/subventions-bpi/); [Acsio](https://www.acsio-conseil.com/aides-innovation-subventions-entreprises-tech-numeriques/); [Winbids Bpifrance](https://www.winbids.fr/sujet/bpifrance/)

### Inferences
- **[estimate]** Time to first € is 2–4 weeks with a fixed fee paid 50% upfront. Success-fee cash arrives 3–9 months later. Realistic 30-day revenue: €0–3,000 (1–2 fixed-fee dossiers from warm startup contacts).
- Edge: Raisup's non-dilutive identification module is her strongest credibility asset. An "éligibilité + dossier BFT/i-Nov" package is a natural fit.
- Legal: no licence is needed to write grant dossiers. For CIR, the fee structure must respect BOFiP doctrine, and liability exposure exists if a credit is later clawed back after a tax audit **[estimate: she should avoid CIR in the first 30 days]**.

## Q4. Offer D: fixed-fee fundraising support (pitch deck + investor list + data room)

### Takeaway
Prices are well established: $2k–8k for pre-seed/seed decks, with freelancers from $300–5k. But the French market in 2026 is **very selective**: deal count fell 10% in H1 2026 while money concentrated in mega-rounds, so early-stage founders are cash-poor. Advisory "conseil en haut de bilan" is free to practise (no licence), whereas soliciting investors (démarchage) is regulated. A fixed-fee "deck + 50 matched investors" package fits Raisup well, and the first sale can land within 30 days through her network.

### Cited Findings
- Pre-seed/seed founders mostly spend $2,000–8,000 on decks. Freelancers charge $300–5,000 per deck (entry $500–1.5k, mid $1.5–3k, senior $3–5k) — [Waveup, pitch deck cost 2026](https://waveup.com/blog/pitch-deck-cost/); [Whitepage Studio](https://www.whitepage.studio/blog/pitch-deck-design-cost); [Next Figures](https://nextfigures.com/blog/pitch-deck-design-cost/)
- Fiverr has a large, low-cost pitch-deck category (price pressure at the low end) — [Fiverr pitch decks](https://www.fiverr.com/categories/business/online-presentations/pitch-decks)
- French startups raised €4.58bn in H1 2026 (+65% year on year), but across only 280 deals (−10%). The average ticket is over €16M, and 7 deals above €100M account for about half the amount (France Digitale/EY barometer) — [ChannelNews](https://www.channelnews.fr/startups-les-levees-de-fonds-bondissent-de-65-au-premier-semestre-158796); [Maddyness, 2026-07-09](https://www.maddyness.com/2026/07/09/levees-de-fonds-ce-que-cache-le-rebond-du-premier-semestre-2026/); [Usine Nouvelle](https://www.usinenouvelle.com/eco-social/economie/start-up/on-a-un-moteur-qui-fonctionne-les-start-up-francaises-ont-leve-46-milliards-deuros-au-premier-semestre-2026-confirmant-le-retour-a-une-dynamique-positive.IZNN5M5OPZDZFHEFEMDZXTJQ3Y.html)
- The AMF treats "conseil en haut de bilan" (advising companies on fundraising and financing) as a related service under art. L.321-2 3° CMF that anyone may practise freely. Démarchage bancaire/financier (soliciting investors) is regulated — [AMF position DOC-2018-03](https://www.amf-france.org/sites/institutionnel/files/private/2024-02/doc-2018-03_vf2_1.pdf)
- Incumbent French offers include SeedLegals advisory, FrenchFunding, Angelsquare and Baker Tilly corporate finance — [SeedLegals](https://seedlegals.com/fr/financement-startup/advisory/); [FrenchFunding](https://frenchfunding.fr/); [Angelsquare](https://home.angelsquare.co/notre-accompagnement/)
- Total fundraising costs are about €60–90k for a €500k round [snippet from a secondary-source search summary; treat with caution] — [Plateya (blocked)](https://www.plateya.fr/blog/detail/levee-de-fonds-combien-coute-vraiment-un-accompagnement)

### Inferences
- **[estimate]** Package "Deck + liste 50 investisseurs matchés + data room checklist" at €990–1,990 fixed, 50% upfront. Time to first € is 1–3 weeks via incubators and her Raisup waitlist. Realistic 30-day revenue: €1,000–4,000.
- Legal guardrail: she should sell the advice and deliverables, and never take a success fee tied to introducing investors or contacting them on the client's behalf (démarchage risk) without taking advice first.
- Risk: startup clients have little cash, "AI-generated deck" can look low-value, and the deal-count contraction shrinks the buyer pool.
