# No-code interactive content builders (calculators, quote estimators, configurators, quizzes) — competitor map, global, as of Sept 2026

Research date: 2026-09-28. Method: web search snippets only. **Every vendor site I tried to fetch directly (outgrow.co, convertcalculator.com, involve.me, allculator.io) was blocked by the network egress proxy**, so all figures below come from search-result snippets (vendor pages, G2/Capterra, Latka, third-party blogs as surfaced in search). Snippets can be stale or taken out of context; treat prices as "as surfaced in Sept 2026 search" and re-verify before publishing. Many "pricing 2026" pages are written by competitors (formbricks.com, involve.me, scoreapp.com, genzform.com, convertsite.com) — flagged where used.

Latka caution: Latka figures are self-reported or modelled "est. ARR" and are sometimes internally inconsistent (see involve.me). Treat as estimates.

## 1. Competitor map (per player)

### Takeaway
The market splits into (a) dedicated calculator/quote builders (Outgrow, involve.me, ConvertCalculator, Calconic, uCalc, Calculoid, Elfsight, Common Ninja, plus new AI-native entrants like ConvertSite, GenZform, Minform, Embeddable, Allculator), (b) quiz/assessment funnel builders (Interact, ScoreApp, Riddle, Perspective, Heyflow, Leadshook), and (c) general form builders with calculation fields (Typeform, Jotform, Tally, Fillout, Zoho Forms, Paperform, Landbot). Entry paid plans cluster at $15–$50/month; branding removal is usually gated to the 2nd tier.

## 2. Does any existing player already do "upload your document / pricing sheet → interactive embeddable tool in 60 seconds"? How well?

### Takeaway
Yes, partially. ConvertSite explicitly markets "upload a spreadsheet, PDF or document" → quote calculator/configurator embed, and Typeform AI accepts file uploads (for forms, not priced calculators). Most others (Outgrow, involve.me, Elfsight, Minform, GenZform, Interact) generate from a prompt or URL, not from a price list. No third-party reviews found evaluating document-to-calculator quality.

### Cited Findings
- ConvertSite: AI assistant creates first version "from your description or files"; upload spreadsheet/PDF/document; then edit calculations and branching logic in visual builder; $25/mo entry with 500 AI credits — [ConvertSite](https://www.convertsite.com/); [ConvertSite pricing](https://www.convertsite.com/pricing).
- Typeform AI: upload files + one prompt to create a form (e.g., job description → screening form); on all current paid plans — [Typeform Help](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/42053773420948-Create-and-edit-forms-with-Typeform-AI).
- Interact: generates a quiz from your website URL (scraping) — [Interact blog](https://www.tryinteract.com/blog/best-ai-quiz-makers-in-2026/).
- Outgrow: prompt or blog URL → quiz; AI calculator via Q&A on industry/audience; manual formula verification still needed — [Outgrow AI](https://outgrow.co/ai/); [Authencio](https://www.authencio.com/blog/outgrow-review-pricing-features-8-best-alternatives).
- involve.me: conversational AI Agent (beta since 31 Jul 2025) builds funnels incl. calculators from prompts; PDF upload not mentioned — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/07/31/3124695/0/en/involve-me-Launches-First-Ever-Conversational-AI-Agent-for-Funnel-Creation.html).

## 3. Price bands and where the market concentrates

### Takeaway
Entry paid plans concentrate at ~$15–$50/month (monthly billing), with ~20–40% annual discounts; branding removal typically requires the 2nd tier (~$40–$100/mo). Budget widgets sit at $2.50–$20; premium/agency tiers jump to $200–$750+.

### Cited Findings
- Budget ($2.50–$20/mo): Common Ninja from $2.50 [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/common-ninja/pricing); Calconic ~$6+ [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/184774/Calconic/pricing/); uCalc $7.20 [review](https://gauravtiwari.org/ucalc-review/); Zoho Forms $12 [aiproductivity](https://aiproductivity.ai/pricing/zoho-forms/); Fillout Starter $15 [Formbricks](https://formbricks.com/blog/fillout-pricing); ConvertCalculator $15–18 [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/convertcalculator); Calculoid $19, GenZform $19 [Calculoid](https://www.calculoid.com/pricing); [GenZform](https://genzform.com/pricing).
- Core ($22–$50/mo): Heyflow $22 [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/heyflow-heyflow/pricing); Paperform $24; ConvertSite $25; Interact $27–39; Typeform $28–39; involve.me $29–49; Jotform $34–39; ScoreApp $29–39; Landbot €32–40; Outgrow Freelancer ~$45 (sources above).
- Upper ($59–$120): Riddle $59, Perspective €47–59, Grow tiers (involve.me $69–99, Heyflow $79), Outgrow Essentials ~$95–115, ConvertCalculator Platinum $120 (sources above).
- Premium/agency: Outgrow Business ~$600–720/mo, Riddle up to $749, ScoreApp up to $799, Interact "most customers $499+" (competitor claim) — sources above.
- Lead/response caps drive upgrades: Typeform 100 responses at Basic; ScoreApp 100; Perspective 100 leads at Base; Interact Lite 500 leads; Outgrow 1,000 leads at entry; Jotform 1,000 submissions; Fillout 1,000 free — sources above.
- Branding removal gating: involve.me (Grow), Fillout (Pro $40), GenZform (Pro $49), Outgrow lower tiers "forced branding" (reviews); Jotform Bronze removes branding at entry — sources above.

### Inferences
- Market concentrates at the $25–$50/mo entry point; pricing power tends to come from lead caps and branding removal rather than features.
- Usage-based models are rare (LeadsHook impressions, Elfsight/Calculoid views).

## 4. Signs of market size (revenue, customers, funding)

### Takeaway
The pure-play interactive-content/calculator/quiz vendors are small: roughly $3–8M est. ARR each (Outgrow ~$7.3M, Interact ~$5.3M, Riddle ~$2.8M), mostly bootstrapped; Heyflow has ~$22M VC. The large revenue pools sit with general form builders (Jotform ~$145M, Typeform ~$150M) that are adding AI and calculation features.

## 5. Weaknesses (review complaints)

### Takeaway
Recurring complaints: price and steep tier jumps (Outgrow, Heyflow, involve.me), lead/response caps and branding locked behind higher tiers, learning curve for formulas/logic (Outgrow, ScoreApp, ConvertCalculator on complex projects), buggy/basic calculators (involve.me), and AI that drafts but doesn't get complex formulas right.

### Cited Findings
- Outgrow: "Expensive", "Learning curve", Business-tier jump, forced branding on low tiers, cluttered editor, advanced formulas need training — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/outgrow/reviews); [Dupple](https://dupple.com/tools/outgrow).
- involve.me: calculator "basic and buggy", editor bugs, key features on top plans, free cap runs out, refund dispute — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/241622/brandquiz/reviews/).
- Heyflow: pricing high, rigid plans, steep tier jumps, funnel/domain caps — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/heyflow-heyflow/reviews).
- ScoreApp: learning curve, time-consuming — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/scoreapp/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons).
- ConvertCalculator: navigation less intuitive on complex projects, few templates, limited mobile formatting controls — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/176575/ConvertCalculator/reviews/).
- Elfsight: exceeding monthly view limit deactivates the widget — [Elfsight Help](https://help.elfsight.com/article/938-pricing).
- Typeform: 100 responses at Basic; "free plan gutted" narratives — [getpricepulse](https://www.getpricepulse.com/companies/typeform-pricing.html).
- AI generation limits: Outgrow AI is "co-pilot, not autopilot"; complex financial formulas need manual verification — [Authencio](https://www.authencio.com/blog/outgrow-review-pricing-features-8-best-alternatives).

### Inferences
- Openings for a new entrant: accurate formula extraction from real price lists, flat pricing without per-lead caps, branding removal at entry tier, modern templates.

### Gaps

- Reddit/Trustpilot threads not surfaced by search; ScoreApp has a Trustpilot page but contents not retrieved — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.scoreapp.com).
