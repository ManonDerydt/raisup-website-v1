## Q1. Dated inventory: which hard deadlines Oct 2026 – 2029 create mass, non-optional software demand, and what changed in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
The biggest mass waves are **e-invoicing / real-time reporting** and **EU product regulation**. E-invoicing covers FR Sept 2027 for SMEs and micro-businesses (10M+ entities), DE Jan 2027/Jan 2028, PL micro Jan 2027, ES Verifactu Jan/Jul 2027, UAE Jan/Jul 2027 and BE (live since Jan 2026). EU product regulation covers CRA reporting (live 11 Sept 2026, full application 11 Dec 2027), the Data Act access-by-design duty (live 12 Sept 2026), the Battery Passport (18 Feb 2027) and the ESPR DPP (textiles around 2028–29). Almost every other headline mandate was **delayed or narrowed in 2025–2026**: AI Act high-risk rules, CSRD/CSDDD, EUDR, the ADA Title II rule, Colorado AI, Verifactu, Poland's KSeF penalties and Utah age verification. The dates that remain firm mostly land on SMEs.

### Cited Findings

**EU AI Act (after the Digital Omnibus)**
- Provisional agreement on 7 May 2026. High-risk obligations are deferred: stand-alone Annex III systems to **2 Dec 2027**, and AI embedded in Annex I regulated products to **2 Aug 2028**. — [Gibson Dunn (snippet; site blocked)](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/); [CSA Labs](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-omnibus-vii-deadline-delay-20260/)
- The **Article 50 transparency obligations were NOT delayed**. They became applicable on **2 Aug 2026**, including disclosure that a user is interacting with AI. — [Goodwin, Aug 2026](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/alerts-technology-dpc-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-now-in-force); [aiactblog.nl](https://www.aiactblog.nl/en/posts/article-50-transparency-deadline-2-august-2026)
- Systems placed on the market before 2 Aug 2026 get a grace period until **2 Dec 2026** for the Art. 50(2) watermarking / machine-readable marking duty. — [Gibson Dunn snippet](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/)
- The omnibus is reported as adopted as **Regulation (EU) 2026/1744**. — [euaiactchecklist.com](https://euaiactchecklist.com/eu-ai-act-digital-omnibus-2026-1744.html); [Usercentrics](https://usercentrics.com/knowledge-hub/eu-ai-act-high-risk-delay-article-50-transparency-consent/) ("Digital Omnibus Now in Force"). Only secondary sources give this number; it is not confirmed on EUR-Lex.
- **Art. 4 AI literacy** was softened to an obligation of means ("reasonable measures"), not removed. The Commission and Member States must publish SME examples on a single platform. — [Lawwwing](https://lawwwing.com/en/digital-ai-omnibus-changes-updates/)
- The omnibus adds SME and small mid-cap relief: simplified technical documentation, proportionate QMS, reduced fine caps and priority sandbox access. — [trail-ml](https://www.trail-ml.com/blog/eu-ai-act-digital-omnibus-changes)
- **GPAI**: the AI Office gained enforcement powers on **2 Aug 2026**, with fines up to 3% of turnover or €15M. Fines for Art. 5 breaches reach €35M or 7%. Fines for high-risk and Art. 50 breaches reach €15M or 3%. — [Lawwwing](https://lawwwing.com/en/digital-ai-omnibus-changes-updates/); [trail-ml](https://www.trail-ml.com/blog/eu-ai-act-digital-omnibus-changes)

**EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)**
- Art. 14 reporting of actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents has been live since **11 Sept 2026**, through the ENISA Single Reporting Platform. It requires an early warning within 24h, a notification within 72h, and a final report within 14 days of a fix (vulnerabilities) or within 1 month (incidents). All remaining obligations apply from **11 Dec 2027**: CE marking, SBOM, support periods and conformity assessment. — [Crowell & Moring](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/its-live-the-cyber-resilience-act-reporting-is-mandatory-as-of-today-11-september-2026); [Freshfields](https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/blogs/technology-quotient/cyber-resilience-act-reporting-obligations-take-effect-on-11-september-2026-102nzmk); [EC](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting)
- Affected population: "over 600,000 companies worldwide." This figure comes from startup PR copy and was not verified. — [Startup Reporter](https://www.startupreporter.eu/craci-cyber-resilience-act-compliance-seed-funding-finland/)
- Readiness: ENISA surveyed 194 SMEs in 31 countries (Feb–Mar 2026). 66% had heard of the CRA. 54% reported little or no understanding of conformity assessment and 42% struggled with documentation. Only about 35% use SBOMs. 142 of 194 asked for financial support. — [ENISA survey PDF](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-06/SME%20CRA%20survey%20report.pdf); [GRC Report](https://www.grcreport.com/post/eu-cybersecurity-survey-finds-smes-aware-of-cyber-resilience-act-but-ill-prepared-for-its-demands-2)
- Two-thirds of the open-source community was unaware of the CRA (older survey). — [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/open-source-unaware-cyber/)
- Vendors: CRACI (Helsinki) raised a €1.4M pre-seed in May 2026 for CI/CD-integrated CRA documentation and vulnerability tracking. Cycode, Aegister and others publish CRA content. — [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/05/19/craci-raises-eur14m-for-eu-cybersecurity-compliance-platform/); [Cycode](https://cycode.com/blog/cyber-resilience-act/)

**EU Data Act**
- Connected products and related services placed on the market from **12 Sept 2026** must make product and related-service data accessible **by design and by default**: free of charge, machine-readable, and directly accessible where feasible. — [Wilson Sonsini](https://www.wsgr.com/en/insights/eu-data-act-september-2026-deadline-what-businesses-need-to-know.html); [Faegre Drinker](https://www.faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2026/9/september-2026-eu-compliance-deadlines-for-connected-product-manufacturers)

**ESPR Digital Product Passport and Battery Passport**
- The **Battery Passport** is mandatory from **18 Feb 2027** for EV batteries, industrial batteries over 2 kWh and LMT batteries. — [Wikipedia: Battery passport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battery_passport); [BASE](https://www.base-batterypassport.com/blog/regulations-4/battery-passport-delegated-acts-timeline-2026-and-beyond-what-still-needs-finalisation-before-go-live-117)

**E-invoicing and real-time reporting**
- **France**: since 1 Sept 2026, all businesses must be able to receive e-invoices, and large and mid-sized companies must issue them and do e-reporting. SMEs and micro-businesses must issue from **1 Sept 2027**. More than 10 million economic actors are concerned. — [economie.gouv / cleartax](https://www.cleartax.com/fr/en/e-invoicing-france); [Urssaf](https://www.urssaf.fr/accueil/actualites/facturation-electronique.html); [Cegid](https://www.cegid.com/fr/facture-electronique-obligatoire/calendrier-facture-electronique/)
- The DGFiP head said on 7 May 2026 that there will be **no penalties from 1 Sept 2026**; contact with businesses comes first. "PDP" was renamed "PA" (Plateforme Agréée), and there were **150+ PAs by June 2026**. — [cleartax](https://www.cleartax.com/fr/en/e-invoicing-france); [erpimplementation.eu](https://www.erpimplementation.eu/en/france-e-invoicing-mandate-2026-erp-compliance-guide/)
- French readiness: only 29% of French SMEs said they were ready (one survey), while another barometer found 72% "certain to be ready". Three weeks after go-live, SMEs report rejected invoices, multiple platforms to deal with, and longer payment delays. — [Daf-Mag](https://www.daf-mag.fr/Thematique/fonction-finance-1242/financements-innovations-2124/Breves/Facturation-electronique-70-des-PME-ne-sont-pas-pretes-pour-465477.htm); [Juriguide, 21 Sept 2026](https://www.juriguide.com/2026/09/21/facturation-electronique-veritable-casse-tete-pme/). The two surveys conflict.
- **Belgium**: structured B2B e-invoicing over Peppol (Peppol BIS / EN 16931) has been mandatory since **1 Jan 2026** for all VAT-registered businesses. — [SPS Commerce](https://www.spscommerce.com/community/articles/e-invoicing-mandates-in-europe-the-2026-business-guide)
- **Germany**: every business has had to receive e-invoices since Jan 2025. Businesses with turnover above €800k must issue from **1 Jan 2027**, and all businesses from **1 Jan 2028**. — [SPS Commerce](https://www.spscommerce.com/community/articles/e-invoicing-mandates-in-europe-the-2026-business-guide)
- **Poland KSeF**: mandatory for businesses above PLN 200M turnover from Feb 2026, most others from Apr 2026, and micro-businesses from **Jan 2027**. On **16 Sept 2026** the Ministry of Finance **deferred penalties to 1 Jan 2028**. — [vatcalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/poland/poland-mandatory-b2b-ksef-e-invoices-delay-to-july-2024/)
- **Spain Verifactu**: delayed by RDL 15/2025 (2 Dec 2025). It now applies from **1 Jan 2027** for corporate-income-tax payers and from **1 Jul 2027** for other businesses and the self-employed. Taxpayers already in SII are excluded. — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/12/tnf-spain-verifactu-invoicing-system-delayed-to-2027.html); [Sovos](https://sovos.com/regulatory-updates/vat/spain-further-postponement-of-verifactu-deadlines-officially-approved/)
- **UAE**: voluntary from 1 Jul 2026. Mandatory from **1 Jan 2027** for turnover of AED 50M or more, and from **1 Jul 2027** for the remaining VAT-registered businesses in scope. — [Hawksford](https://www.hawksford.com/insights-and-guides/uae-e-invoicing); [cleartax AE](https://www.cleartax.com/ae/e-invoicing-uae)
- **Malaysia**: Phase 4 (turnover up to RM5M) started Jan 2026, with a relaxation period to **31 Dec 2027**. The exemption threshold rose from RM1M to **RM3M effective 1 Sept 2026**. — [cleartax MY](https://www.cleartax.com/my/en/different-phases-implementation-timelines-einvoicing-malaysia); [info-tech.com.my](https://www.info-tech.com.my/blog/malaysia-new-e-invoice-guidelines/)
- Vendor funding: DDD Invoices (Slovenia) raised a €1.31M seed in May 2026 for an e-invoicing API. — [FinTech Global](https://fintech.global/2026/05/14/ddd-invoices-raises-e1-31m-seed-for-e-invoicing-api/)

**Accessibility**
- The **European Accessibility Act** has applied since 28 June 2025. In June 2026 a French court ordered Carrefour France to make its site and app accessible within 6 months, with daily penalties for delay. No confirmed EAA fines could be verified as of June 2026. Maximum fines range from about €60k (Ireland) to about €900k (Sweden). Sweden's PTS received 124 complaints and opened 28 investigations on its own initiative. Micro-enterprises (fewer than 10 staff and €2M or less) are exempt for services only. — [LI Solutions](https://li.solutions/blog/eaa-enforcement-2026/); [auditsu](https://auditsu.com/resources/eaa-enforcement-2026); [webyes](https://www.webyes.com/blogs/eaa-fines/)
- **US ADA Title II**: a DOJ interim final rule of **20 Apr 2026** delayed the deadlines by one year. Public entities with 50k+ population must comply by **26 Apr 2027**, and smaller entities and special districts by **26 Apr 2028**. The WCAG 2.1 AA standard is unchanged. DOJ cited, among other reasons, the limits of generative AI for remediation. An HHS Section 504 deadline of May 2026 was still in force. — [Duane Morris](https://www.duanemorris.com/alerts/doj_extends_ada_title_ii_digital_accessibility_deadlines_one_year_0426.html); [Jackson Lewis](https://www.jacksonlewis.com/insights/doj-extends-public-entities-compliance-deadline-ada-related-website-accessibility-hhss-may-2026-deadline-still-looms)

**Sustainability and supply chain**
- **EUDR**: large and medium operators must comply from **30 Dec 2026**, and micro and small operators (non-timber) from **30 Jun 2027**. The Commission's simplification review of 4 May 2026 confirmed **no further postponement**. Micro and small primary operators need only a one-off simplified declaration. — [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/eu-deforestation-regulation-commission-publishes-simplification-package-ahead-of-december-2026); [IntegrityNext](https://www.integritynext.com/resources/blog/article/eudr-review-out-now-minor-simplifications-same-deadline); [EP](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20251211IPR32168/deforestation-law-parliament-adopts-changes-to-postpone-and-simplify-measures)
- **CSRD/CSDDD (Omnibus I, Directive (EU) 2026/470, OJ 26 Feb 2026)**: CSRD now covers only companies with **more than 1,000 employees and more than €450M turnover**, with first application for financial years from 1 Jan 2027. CSDDD covers companies with more than 5,000 employees and more than €1.5B turnover; transposition is due 26 Jul 2028 and application from **26 Jul 2029**. **The SME market has effectively disappeared.** — [Latham](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/eu-sustainability-omnibus-published-in-the-official-journal); [DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2026/eu-council-approves-omnibus-i-directive)
- **CBAM**: the definitive period started **1 Jan 2026**. A 50-tonne annual de minimis applies, and authorised-declarant status is needed above it. Certificate sales start **1 Feb 2027**, and the first surrender, for 2026 imports, is due by **30 Sept 2027**. The quarterly holding requirement is 50% (reduced from 80%). — [Sprih](https://www.sprih.com/blogs/cbam-compliance-2026-definitive-phase/); [EC TAXUD](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/cbam-definitive-regime_en); [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/eu-adopts-cbam-omnibus-regulation)

Cybersecurity audit certifications are due **1 Apr 2028** (turnover above $100M), **2029** ($50–100M) and **2030** (below $50M). — [Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2025/10/california-finalizes-cppa-regulations); [MoFo](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/251007-ccpa-regulations-on-cybersecurity-risk-assessments)

**Pay transparency and post-quantum cryptography**
- **EU Pay Transparency Directive**: transposition was due **7 June 2026**, and only IT, LT, MT and SK met the deadline. NL, SE, CZ and DK have confirmed an effective date of **1 Jan 2027**. - The **strongest "position before the wave" bets** for a small team are:
  - CRA: December 2027 is fixed, SME readiness is low, and every software or IoT SMB selling in the EU is affected.
  - AI content labelling / transparency (EU Art. 50 plus California SB 942): already live, and one global SDK can serve both.
  - The ESPR textile DPP: around 2028–29, with a long tail of SMB brands.
- E-invoicing is the largest wave by count, but it is **already crowded** (150+ French PAs). SMB opportunities are more likely in thin layers on top: rejection and reconciliation handling, and multi-country API for small SaaS.
- CSRD and CSDDD have collapsed as SMB markets after Omnibus I. Pivot away from them.
- The main risk is delay. In 2025–26, regulators repeatedly deferred penalties or dates (PL, FR no-penalty, ES, ADA, CO, Utah, AI Act high-risk), so revenue models should not rely on the first deadline.
